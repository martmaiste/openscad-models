# OSRAM Nightlux Stair Wall Mount

![OSRAM Nightlux Stair Wall Mount rendered in OpenSCAD](OSRAM-Nightlux-Stair-Mount.png)

A parametric, 3D-printable slide-in wall/door mount for the **OSRAM Nightlux Stair LED Light**. 

This design fixes the issue where the original magnetic mount is too weak, causing the light to fall off when mounted on a frequently slammed door or wall.

## Features

- **Side-Sliding Design**: A robust C-channel profile. Slide the light in from the left or right side.
- **Magnetic Auto-Centering**: A circular recess allows the light's built-in magnet to snap into place and naturally center the light in the track.
- **Zero-Support Printability**: Print standing upright on one of its open side ends for flawless results with **zero supports**.
- **Countersunk Screw Holes**: Four screw holes (two vertical, two horizontal) for standard **3mm countersunk screws**. Screws are hidden behind the light body.

---

## 3D Printing

### Recommended Orientation
- **Print Upright (On its side end)**: Rotate the mount 90° so it lies flat on its left or right open end. This prevents layer separation on the lips and requires **no supports**.

### Print Settings
- **Material**: **PETG** (Recommended), **PLA**, or **ABS/ASA**.
- **Layer Height**: `0.20 mm` to `0.28 mm`
- **Perimeters/Walls**: **3-4 walls minimum**.
- **Infill**: `15%` to `20%`.
- **Supports**: **None** (if printed on its side end).

---

## Installation

1. Mark the mounting position on your door or wall.
2. Screw the wall mount into your door/wall using **3mm countersunk wood screws** (choose vertical or horizontal holes).
3. Slide the OSRAM light sideways into the track until the magnet snaps into the circular recess.
