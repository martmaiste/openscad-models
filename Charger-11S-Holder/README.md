create a parametric openscad design for a wall mount for a charger. Use directory name as a filename and use versioning starting from v0.01 in the content. the chager is made from cast aluminium with cooling fins and ventilator and the base plate is a 1.6mm aluminium sheet that is larger than the cast aluminium part by 4mm both on top and the bottom. the holder should be 68mm wide and 116mm high with 4 mounting holes for 4mm wood screws. The holder base plate is attached to the wall and on bottom and top there is a extuding part. the bottom part should have a 2mm deep slot and top part 4mm slot so that one can slide the top fully to the slot, then the bottom part will fit in and moved down to rest in the 2mm slot. since the bottom slot is only 2mm deep then in the resting position there is extra 2mm on the top to hold the charger. keep the chargers pase plate 5mm away from the holder so that circulating air can cool the back of the charger.
